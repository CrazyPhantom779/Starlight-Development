# Wizard balance sheet (v3)

Wind: 100 max, 1.6/s base. Each Mastery point: +4 max, +0.06/s. Wounds slow regeneration down to 20%. Tides move each school ×0.8-1.3 per wizard.
Cooldown of a woven spell = cost × 0.12 s (1.5-24 s). Casts vary ±6% (more with Instability).

Anchors used: ~1.2 damage per Wind (single target), control ≈ 8 Wind per second of stun, healing ≈ 1.7 HP per Wind.
